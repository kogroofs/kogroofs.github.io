Seamless gutters

5- and 6-inch gutters for homes, 7-inch for commercial buildings. Updated October 2026.

The short answer

We install seamless gutters, formed on site to fit your building: 5-inch and 6-inch gutters for homes, and 7-inch for commercial buildings. They run $5.50 to $10 a foot, installed with a new roof or on their own.

Seamless, not sectional

Sectional gutters come in short pieces joined at seams, and the seams are where they rust and leak. Seamless gutters are cut to length on site from one continuous piece, so a run has joints only at the corners and downspouts. Fewer joints means fewer places to leak, and no seams to re-caulk every few years.

Which size

  • 5-inch. The common size on homes, and plenty for many roofs.
  • 6-inch. Carries about 40 percent more water than a 5-inch gutter. We recommend it for long runs, steep roofs, valleys that dump a lot of water into one spot, and lower gutters that catch water from an upper roof. It's usually paired with larger 3-by-4-inch downspouts.
  • 7-inch. For commercial buildings, where big roof areas drain to a few long runs.

Texas storms can drop a lot of rain fast, and an undersized gutter overflows exactly when you need it most. We size gutters to the roof area that drains into them, not just to what was there before.

Gutter styles

  • K-style. The most common gutter on homes built in the last 50 years. The flat back fastens straight to the fascia board, and it carries more water than a half-round gutter of the same width.
  • Half-round. The rounded, traditional look on older homes. It usually hangs on brackets, and its open shape clogs more easily, so it's often paired with leaf guards.
  • Box gutters. Large rectangular gutters on commercial buildings, sometimes built into the roof edge. They move a lot of water but need regular upkeep to keep from leaking.

Materials

  • Aluminum. Light, won't rust, and comes painted in many colors. The most common choice for seamless gutters. It can dent from ladders and hail.
  • Steel. Very strong, but heavy, and galvanized steel can rust over the years.
  • Copper. Can last a very long time and never needs paint, but costs several times more.
  • Vinyl. Cheap, but it can get brittle and fade in strong sun, which is a hard sell in a Texas summer.

What makes gutters work

  • Slope. About 1/4 inch of fall for every 10 feet of gutter, so water runs to the downspouts instead of standing.
  • Enough downspouts, in the right places. A long run needs more than one, and corners where valleys dump water need one close by.
  • Water carried away from the house. Downspout extensions or splash blocks keep water from pooling against the foundation, where North Texas clay soil swells when it's wet and shrinks when it dries.
  • Solid fastening. Hangers screwed into sound fascia board. Rotten fascia gets replaced first, or the new gutter won't stay put.

Gutter guards

Screens, micro-mesh, and covers can cut down on cleaning under trees. None of them is maintenance-free, and the right one depends on what's falling on your roof. If you're thinking about guards, we'll tell you honestly whether they're worth it for your house.

Common questions

Should I replace my gutters when I get a new roof?

It's often the best time. The drip edge and the gutters meet at the eaves, and doing both together means one crew, one cleanup, and a clean fit. If your gutters are sound and the right size, we'll leave them and tell you so.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

At least once or twice a year, and more often under trees. Clogged gutters overflow at the worst spots: over doors, onto the foundation, and behind the gutter into the fascia board.

Do gutter guards stop all cleaning?

No guard is maintenance-free. Screens and covers cut down on leaves, but fine debris and roof granules can still build up, and some guards make the gutter harder to clean when it does need it.

Can hail damage gutters?

Yes. Dents in gutters and downspouts are one of the first signs hail hit a home. We include them in a storm inspection report, with photos.
