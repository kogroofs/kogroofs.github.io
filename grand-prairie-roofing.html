Permits in Grand Prairie

Grand Prairie requires a residential re-roof permit for any roof replacement, re-cover, or repair, not just a full tear-off. Contractors have to be registered with the city as general contractors, and the city says permits typically take 5 to 10 business days to process.

Inspections follow the 2021 International Residential Code, and work can't go past an inspection point until it's approved. We request every inspection through the city's portal and keep your job on schedule around them. The city's Building Inspections office is at 972-237-8230.

Grand Prairie stretches across county lines, from Dallas County into Tarrant County, so homes a few miles apart can sit in different counties with different appraisal districts. The city's permit rules are the same on both sides.
