Permits in Arlington

Arlington requires a building permit to re-roof, unless the repair covers 10 percent of the roof or less. Every contractor has to be registered with the city, and the permit has to be posted at the house before work starts.

  • Tear-off to the deck. The old roof comes off down to the sheathing in most cases, and a re-cover isn't allowed over two or more existing layers or over a wet or deteriorated roof.
  • New underlayment, drip edge, and flashing. Underlayment is replaced when you re-shingle, and flashing and drip edge are required.
  • Decking. Damaged decking is replaced with at least 7/16-inch sheathing, and new decking can't be laid over old.
  • Low slopes. Shingles aren't allowed below a 2:12 pitch, and pitches from 2:12 to 4:12 need two layers of underlayment.
  • Final inspection. The city does a final inspection. A homeowner can waive the attic part with a signed, notarized affidavit.
  • Torch-down roofs. Applying roofing with an open flame needs a separate operational permit from the Arlington Fire Department, which matters on flat commercial roofs.
