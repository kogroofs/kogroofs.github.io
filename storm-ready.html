KOG Storm Ready: get your roof on record before hail season

Free for homes and commercial buildings across Dallas-Fort Worth

The short answer

KOG Storm Ready is free. Before hail season, we inspect your roof and photograph it, so there's a dated record of its condition before the storms come. When radar shows hail 1 inch or larger near your address, we let you know, and we come back for a free re-check against those photos.

What you get

  • A free roof inspection before storm season, with a dated written report and photos that we keep on file.
  • Help finding your deductible and roof coverage on your policy's declarations page, so the number doesn't surprise you after a storm. Your insurance agent explains what your policy covers. If the deductible is more than you can pay at once, our deductible payment plan spreads it out.
  • A hail alert when NOAA radar shows hail 1 inch or larger near your address.
  • A free re-check after the storm, compared against your before photos.
  • Priority tarping if a storm opens up your roof.

Why "before" photos matter

After a storm, the hardest question is what the roof looked like before it. A dated report from before hail season shows what's new and what isn't. That helps you decide whether a storm did real damage, and it gives you a clear before-and-after to show your insurance company if it did.

Storm Ready doesn't guarantee that an insurance company will pay for anything. Your policy and your insurance company decide that.

What it costs

Nothing. There's no contract and no catch. If the inspection finds something that needs fixing now, we'll tell you, show you the photos, and give you a written price. What you do with it is up to you.
