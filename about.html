About KOG Roofing

Owned and run by William "Bobby" Roach since 2010

The short answer

KOG Roofing is owned and run by William "Bobby" Roach. He started KOG in 2010, it has been registered in Texas since 2011, and it repairs and replaces roofs on homes and commercial buildings across Dallas-Fort Worth from its base in Granbury.

Bobby's story

Bobby started KOG in 2010 after knocking doors for another Dallas-Fort Worth roofer. He printed his own contracts and business cards and sold his first roof at sundown on his first day out. KOG has been registered in Texas since 2011, and Bobby has roofed across DFW for 13 years without a single complaint. KOG holds an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau.

He's a roofer first: shingle, metal, TPO, EPDM, and torch-down roofs, plus seamless gutters and siding, with manufacturer training from GAF, Owens Corning, and Malarkey. Going back to roofs he'd put on years earlier and seeing how the shingles aged is what turned him toward metal.

Bobby has also spent years in the oilfield as a designated safety leader, running JSAs, safety meetings, and incident investigations. He brings that same discipline to every commercial and industrial job.

"You want to buy a roof from a roofer or a salesman? Me, I'm a roofer."

How KOG works

  • Free inspections with a written report. Photos, what we found, and what it needs. If your roof doesn't need anything, we'll tell you and show you why.
  • Bobby runs the job. The inspection, the estimate, the material order, and the crew schedule.
  • Prices you can see first. Our price ranges are posted on the site.
  • Roof to interior. When a leak damages ceilings and walls, KOG handles the inside repairs too.
  • Emergency tarping, day or night. If water is coming in, call any time.
