Prices

The KOG Straight Deal: pay KOG nothing until your new roof is on

For new roofs you pay for yourself, with no insurance claim. Dallas-Fort Worth.

The short answer

When you pay for a new roof yourself, you pay KOG nothing until the roof is on. Your only money up front goes to the supply house for your materials, at our price with no markup, and you get the receipt. Your price is in writing and doesn't change. If we find rotten decking, the first 2 sheets are free. And your first year of the Roof Care Plan is on us.

What you get

  • You pay KOG after the roof is on. Not before. You pay us once the roof is finished and you've walked it with us.
  • Materials at our price, with the receipt. Your materials payment goes straight to the supply house, not to KOG, and you see exactly what they cost.
  • A written price that doesn't change. It shows your materials and KOG's price for everything else: tear-off, labor, cleanup, haul-off, and the warranty. No add-ons on tear-off day.
  • The first 2 sheets of rotten decking free. After that, $95 per 4x8 sheet, with photos of every sheet we replace.
  • The same price, cash or financed. We don't fold lender fees into your price. If you want monthly payments, take our written price to your own bank or credit union.
  • Your first year of the Roof Care Plan free. A $199 value: a yearly inspection, a roof check after big hail, priority emergency service, and 10% off repairs.
  • Paperwork for an insurance discount. Choose a Class 4 impact-resistant or metal roof, and we give you the install paperwork your insurance company asks for. Discounts vary by company, and none is guaranteed, so ask your agent first.
  • A real warranty. Five years on labor for roofs 4/12 and steeper, one year on low-slope roofs, plus the manufacturer's material warranty.

How it works

  1. Free inspection and a written price

    We measure the roof, photograph it, and give you the price in writing, with the materials and KOG's part shown separately.

  2. You sign and pay for your materials

    Your materials payment goes straight to the supply house. We tell you exactly how to pay them, and you get the receipt.

  3. We tear off and build your new roof

    You get photos of the bare deck and of any decking we replace.

  4. Walk it with us, then pay KOG

    Once the roof is done and cleaned up, we walk it together. Then you pay KOG.

The neighbor deal: $500 off for each of you

If a neighbor on your street signs for a new KOG roof they pay for themselves before your roof is paid for, you each get $500 off. One crew, one street, one trip, and you both save.

One discount per roof: the neighbor deal or the KOG100 coupon, not both. We may end this offer at any time.

Why we do it this way

The worst roofing stories start with a big deposit paid to a roofer who never comes back. With the Straight Deal, your materials money goes to the supply house, and KOG's money comes after the work is done. If we don't finish, we don't get paid.

A lot of "0% interest" roof offers are lender loans. Miss the payoff date and the interest can hit the whole original balance, and the lender's fee to the roofer often ends up in your price. We'd rather post our prices and keep them the same however you pay.

What it covers

The Straight Deal is for shingle and metal roof replacements on homes, paid for by the homeowner with no insurance claim. For a ballpark on your home, try the roof cost calculator.

Questions

Do I pay KOG a deposit for a new roof?

No. On a Straight Deal roof, your only money up front is for your materials, and it goes straight to the supply house, not to KOG. You pay KOG after the roof is on and you've walked it with us.

How do you charge for rotten decking?

The first 2 sheets are free. After that, it's $95 per 4x8 sheet, and you get photos of every sheet we replace. If the whole roof needs new decking, like an older home with board decking and wide gaps, we price that before work starts.

Do you mark up the materials?

No. You pay the supply house our price for your materials and you get the receipt. KOG's price covers everything else: tear-off, labor, cleanup, haul-off, and the warranty.

Can I finance a new roof?

You can take our written price to your own bank or credit union for a home improvement loan, and we'll give them the paperwork they need. Your price is the same whether you pay cash or borrow. We don't add lender fees to it.

Does the Straight Deal work with an insurance claim?

No. It's for roofs you pay for yourself. On an insurance claim, the scope your insurance company approves sets the price and you pay your deductible, as Texas law requires. If the deductible is hard to pay at once, ask about our deductible payment plan.

What's your warranty?

Five years on labor for roofs 4/12 and steeper, one year on low-slope roofs, plus the manufacturer's material warranty.
