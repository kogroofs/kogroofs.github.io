The KOG Straight Deal on a new roof

2 sheets of decking free. Your first year of roof care free. Up to 10 years on labor. For roofs you pay for yourself in Dallas-Fort Worth.

The short answer

When you pay for a new roof yourself, the first 2 sheets of rotten decking are free, your first year of the Roof Care Plan is free, and your labor warranty keeps going for up to 10 years as long as you keep the plan. Your price is in writing, and nothing gets added unless you see the photos and approve it first. You pay for your materials when you sign, and the rest the day your roof is done.

What you get

  • The first 2 sheets of rotten decking free. After that, $95 per 4x8 sheet for up to 5 more, with photos of every sheet we replace. If your roof needs more than that, we stop, show you the photos, and give you a written price for the rest before we replace another board.
  • Your first year of the Roof Care Plan free. A $199 value: a yearly inspection, a roof check after big hail, priority emergency service, and 10% off repairs.
  • Five years on labor, and we mean it. No fine print that turns your warranty into a repair bill. Keep the Roof Care Plan, and your labor warranty keeps going, up to 10 years. Plus the manufacturer's material warranty.
  • A written price, and no surprise bills. It covers everything: materials, tear-off, labor, cleanup, haul-off, and the warranty. Some things only show up once the old roof is off. If we find one, you get photos and a written price before we fix it, and nothing extra gets done until you approve it.
  • No big deposit for labor. You pay for your materials when you sign, and you see them delivered to your driveway before any work starts. You pay the rest the day your roof is done and you've walked it with us.
  • The same price, cash or financed. If you want monthly payments, take our written price to your own bank or credit union. Your price doesn't go up because you borrowed.
  • Paperwork for an insurance discount. Choose a Class 4 impact-resistant or metal roof, and we give you the install paperwork your insurance company asks for. Discounts vary by company, and none is guaranteed, so ask your agent first.

How it works

  1. Free inspection and a written price

    We measure the roof, photograph it, and give you the price in writing.

  2. You sign and pay for your materials

    Your materials are ordered and delivered to your driveway before any work starts.

  3. We tear off and build your new roof

    You get photos of the bare deck and of any decking we replace. If we find anything else, you see it and approve the price before we fix it.

  4. Walk it with us, then pay the rest

    Once the roof is done and cleaned up, we walk it together. Then you pay the rest, and your first year of the Roof Care Plan starts.

Why we do it this way

A 20-year or lifetime labor warranty sounds great until the roof leaks in year 12 and the fine print calls it wear and tear. In Texas, the sun ages a shingle roof fast. We'd rather give you a warranty we'll actually stand behind, and keep it going for as long as we're on your roof every year.

The worst roofing stories start with a big deposit paid to a roofer who never comes back. With the Straight Deal, your first payment buys your materials, and you see them in your driveway before work starts. KOG gets paid for the work when the work is done.

A lot of "0% interest" roof offers are lender loans. Miss the payoff date and the interest can hit the whole original balance, and the lender's fee to the roofer often ends up in your price. We'd rather post our prices and keep them the same however you pay.

What it covers

The Straight Deal is for shingle and metal roof replacements on homes, paid for by the homeowner with no insurance claim. For a ballpark on your home, try the roof cost calculator.

Questions

How do you charge for rotten decking?

The first 2 sheets are free. After that, it's $95 per 4x8 sheet for up to 5 more, and you get photos of every sheet we replace. If your roof needs more than that, we stop, show you the photos, and give you a written price for the rest before we replace another board. If we can tell before the job that the whole roof needs new decking, like an older home with board decking and wide gaps, it's in your written price from the start.

What's your warranty?

Five years on labor for roofs 4/12 and steeper, and as long as you keep the Roof Care Plan, it keeps going, up to 10 years. Low-slope roofs get one year. It covers our installation. It doesn't cover storm damage, the normal aging of the materials (that's the manufacturer's warranty), or damage from anyone else working on the roof. Ask any roofer offering 20 years what theirs doesn't cover.

What if you find something else when the old roof comes off?

Some things don't show until the old roof is off, like rotted fascia, more rotten decking, or an old layer hiding under the top one. When that happens, we stop, show you photos, and give you a written price for the fix. Nothing extra gets done or billed until you approve it.

Do I pay a deposit for a new roof?

Only for your materials. You pay for them when you sign, and you see them delivered to your driveway before work starts. You pay the rest the day your roof is done and you've walked it with us.

Can I finance a new roof?

You can take our written price to your own bank or credit union for a home improvement loan, and we'll give them the paperwork they need. Your price is the same whether you pay cash or borrow.

Does the Straight Deal work with an insurance claim?

No. It's for roofs you pay for yourself. On an insurance claim, the scope your insurance company approves sets the price and you pay your deductible, as Texas law requires. If the deductible is hard to pay at once, ask about our deductible payment plan.
