How a job goes

  1. Inspection

    We get on the roof, and in the attic when needed, and take photos and video of what we find.

  2. Written report

    Photos, the cause of the problem, and whether it calls for a repair or a replacement.

  3. Your decision

    Take your time. The report is yours to keep either way.

  4. Written contract

    Scope, price, schedule, and warranty, spelled out before any work starts.

  5. The work

    Daily cleanup, and a magnetic sweep of the yard, driveway, or parking lot when we finish.

  6. Warranty

    Five years on labor for roofs 4/12 and steeper, one year on low-slope roofs, plus the manufacturer's material warranty.

About KOG Roofing

Bobby Roach started KOG Roofing in 2010 with a stack of work orders and business cards, and sold his first roof at sundown on his first day. Since then he has sold and run hundreds of jobs, from shingle re-roofs to TPO, EPDM, torch-down, and metal.

He runs each job himself: the inspection, the estimate, the material order, and the crew schedule. His favorite part of the work hasn't changed. It's telling an owner the truth about their roof, finishing the job right, and driving home knowing they're happy.
