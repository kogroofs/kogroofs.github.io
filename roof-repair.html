The short answer

Most leaks start in one place. We find where the water is getting in, fix that, and show you photos of the problem and the repair. If the roof is worn out and a repair would be money wasted, we'll tell you that too.

What we repair

Shingle roofs

  • Pipe boots. The rubber collars around plumbing vents dry out and crack in the Texas sun. It's one of the most common leaks we find.
  • Flashing. Where the roof meets a wall, a chimney, or a skylight, loose or rusted flashing lets water run behind it.
  • Valleys. Where two slopes meet, water runs fast and heavy, and worn valleys leak first.
  • Wind damage. Missing shingles, creased shingles that lifted and folded back, and loose ridge caps.
  • Nail pops and exposed nails that let water down the shank and into the deck.

Flat and low-slope roofs

  • TPO. Split or open seams, punctures, and failed flashing at walls and curbs.
  • EPDM rubber. Shrinkage that pulls the membrane away from edges and corners, and seam failures.
  • Torch-down modified bitumen. Cracks, blisters, and open laps.
  • Ponding water that sits for days and wears out the membrane, and clogged drains and scuppers.
  • Rooftop units. Leaks around HVAC curbs, vents, and other penetrations.

Metal roofs

  • Exposed-fastener panels. Backed-out screws and worn rubber washers, one of the most common metal roof leaks.
  • Seams, trim, and flashing on standing seam and panel roofs.

How a repair visit works

  1. Inspection. We get on the roof, and in the attic when needed, to trace the water back to where it's getting in. It doesn't always come in where the stain shows up.
  2. Photos and an honest answer. You see what we found. If it's a repair, we say so. If the roof is worn out, we show you why.
  3. Written price. You get the price in writing before any work starts.
  4. The repair. We fix the cause, not just the symptom, and clean up after.
  5. After photos. You see the finished repair, even the parts you can't see from the ground.

Signs you need a roof repair

  • Water stains on a ceiling or wall, or a musty smell in the attic
  • Missing, creased, or lifted shingles after wind
  • Shingle granules piling up in the gutters
  • Puddles that stand on a flat roof for days after rain
  • Rust, loose screws, or lifted panels on a metal roof

Common questions

How much does a roof repair cost?

It depends on what failed and how hard it is to reach. A cracked pipe boot is a small job; a leak hidden under several layers takes more. Either way, you get a written price before we start.

Do you repair roofs another company installed?

Yes. Most of the leaks we fix are on roofs someone else put on.

Can hail damage be repaired, or does it need a new roof?

It depends on how widespread the damage is. A few damaged spots can often be repaired. Damage across the whole roof usually means replacement, and that's often an insurance claim. Read After a hail or wind storm for what to do first.

Do you repair commercial roofs?

Yes. Flat and metal roofs on shops, warehouses, offices, and churches are a big part of our work. See commercial flat roofing.

What area do you cover?

All of Dallas-Fort Worth, across the twelve counties on our service area list.

Got a leak?

Call and tell us what you're seeing. If water is coming in right now, we'll tell you how to limit the damage until we get there.
