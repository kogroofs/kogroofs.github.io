When hail hits Weatherford

Most of the big-hail days near Weatherford since 2023, the days with stones 1 inch or larger, came between March and June. May has been the worst single month. But it has also come in August, September, October, and November, so a storm outside spring is worth a look too.

The most common time has been in the evening, between 6 PM and midnight, followed by the afternoon. Several storms hit overnight, while most people were asleep. A few storms hit in the morning. If a storm woke you up, or you found hail in the yard the next morning, check your address against the radar.

Permits in Weatherford

The City of Weatherford lists roof replacement and major roof repairs among the work that typically needs a building permit, while small repairs usually don't. The fee depends on the scope of the job. We pull the permit before a replacement starts. The city's permit office is at (817) 598-4282, option 2.

Weatherford is the county seat of Parker County. Homes out in the county, beyond the city limits, follow different permit rules than homes in town.
