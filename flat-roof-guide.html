TPO, EPDM, or torch-down? Flat roof options for commercial buildings

Updated October 2026

The short answer

All three are proven low-slope roofs. TPO is a white, heat-welded membrane that reflects the sun. EPDM is a rubber membrane with a long track record. Torch-down modified bitumen is a layered asphalt system that stands up well to foot traffic. The right choice depends on your building, its drainage, how much traffic the roof sees, and your budget.

A flat roof still has to drain

Low-slope roofs aren't truly flat. They need enough slope, plus drains and scuppers, to move water off. Water that is still sitting about 48 hours after rain is called ponding, and it shortens the life of any membrane.

If your roof ponds, fix the drainage as part of the job. Tapered insulation, added drains, or crickets behind rooftop units can move the water where it needs to go.

Tear-off or recover?

Sometimes a new membrane can go over the existing roof, which saves the cost of tearing it off. That only works when the existing roof and insulation are dry and sound, and building codes generally allow it over just one existing roof.

Wet insulation has to come out, or the new roof will fail from underneath. Core cuts or a moisture scan show what's under the surface before you decide.

Keep any flat roof working longer

  • Inspect it twice a year, in spring and fall, and after major storms.
  • Keep drains, gutters, and scuppers clear of leaves and debris.
  • Watch the penetrations. HVAC curbs, pipes, vents, and skylights are where most flat roofs leak.
  • Limit foot traffic, and check the roof after other trades have been up there.
  • Fix small punctures and open seams right away, before water reaches the insulation.
  • Keep inspection and repair records. Many manufacturer warranties expect them.

A note on torch-down safety

Torch-applied systems use an open flame. On occupied buildings, or near wood and other things that burn, self-adhered or cold-applied modified bitumen can do the same job without a torch. Ask your roofer which method they plan to use and why.
