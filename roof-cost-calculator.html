How we figure it

  1. The footprint. Your living area divided by the number of stories, plus the garage: about 260 square feet for a 1-car garage, 480 for a 2-car, and 700 for a 3-car.
  2. The overhangs. We add 10 percent, because the roof hangs past the walls.
  3. The slope. A sloped roof has more surface than the ground under it. We multiply by 1.07 for a low roof, 1.16 for medium, and 1.35 for steep.
  4. The price. The squares of roof, times our price per square: $500–$750 for architectural shingles, $645–$860 for exposed-fastener metal panels, and $995–$1,325 for standing seam.

For example, take a one-story, 2,000-square-foot home with a 2-car garage and a medium roof. That's 2,480 square feet of footprint, times 1.10, times 1.16, or about 32 squares. Architectural shingles come to roughly $16,000 to $24,000.

What can move the price

  • Rotten or soft decking that has to be replaced, at $95 a sheet. On a roof you pay for yourself, the first 2 sheets are free with the Straight Deal.
  • More than one old layer to tear off
  • A two-story roof, a very steep roof, or a lot of valleys and hips
  • Chimneys, skylights, and walls that need new flashing
  • Upgrades like Class 4 impact-resistant shingles
  • Ventilation work, like adding soffit vents or a ridge vent. Read why attic ventilation matters.

If insurance is paying

When a claim is paying for the roof, the scope your insurance company approves sets the price, and you pay your deductible. Texas law requires that, and a calculator doesn't change it. To see your share of a claim, use our roof claim calculator, or read our insurance questions.

Common questions

What's a square?

Roofers price by the square, which is 100 square feet of roof. A 32-square roof has about 3,200 square feet of surface.

Why is my roof bigger than my house?

The roof is sloped, it hangs past the walls, and it covers the garage too. With the garage and the overhangs, a one-story home's roof is often 25 to 60 percent bigger than its living area.

How close is the ballpark?

Close enough to plan with. Odd-shaped roofs, big porches, and very steep pitches throw it off. That's why your written price comes after we measure the roof itself.

Can I price a commercial roof here?

No. Flat and commercial roofs depend on the deck, the drainage, and the insulation, so we price them after an inspection. See commercial roofing.
