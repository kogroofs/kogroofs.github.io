Luxury roofing in Dallas-Fort Worth

Composite slate and shake, standing seam, stone-coated steel, and cedar for high-end homes

The short answer

A high-end roof has to look right from the street and stand up to North Texas hail. We install composite slate and shake from makers like DaVinci and Brava, standing seam metal, stone-coated steel like DECRA, and new cedar shakes. Every job starts with a free inspection, a written report with photos, and a written price.

The roofs we install

Composite slate and shake

Composite tiles are molded to look like natural slate or hand-split cedar, without the weight of stone or the upkeep of wood. DaVinci rates its composite slate and shake Class A for fire and Class 4 for impact. Brava's tiles are rated Class 4 for impact and come in Class A and Class C fire versions, so ask for Class A.

They need a solid deck. DaVinci requires solid wood panel or lumber sheathing, so on a home that had wood shakes over spaced boards, we tear off the wood and put down a new plywood deck first.

Ballpark: $1,100 to $2,000 a square, torn off and installed, before deck work. The product line, the roof's shape, and the trim and flashing move it within that range. Your written price comes after we measure.

Standing seam metal

Panels lock together at raised seams, with the fasteners hidden under clips, for clean lines and no exposed screws to wear out. At our posted prices, standing seam runs $995 to $1,325 a square, torn off and installed. A square is 100 square feet of roof. See metal roofing, or price it on your home with the metal roof cost calculator.

Stone-coated steel

Steel panels coated in stone granules and shaped to look like shake, tile, or shingles. DECRA rates its stone-coated steel Class 4 for impact and Class A for fire, and its lifetime limited warranty covers hail penetration from stones up to 2.5 inches.

Ballpark: $900 to $1,500 a square, torn off and installed, before deck work.

Cedar shakes

Nothing looks quite like real cedar, and we install new cedar shake roofs. Some insurance companies won't insure or renew wood roofs, so get your company's answer in writing before you choose wood. Read insurance dropping you over a wood shake roof?

Natural slate, clay or concrete tile, or copper details? Our crews install those too. Ask us.

What makes a high-end roof last

The tiles get the attention, but most roof problems start in the parts you don't see. On a luxury roof, these matter as much as the product on top:

  • Flashing. Valleys, walls, chimneys, and skylights are where leaks often start. They get new flashing, fitted to the roof.
  • The deck. Composite tiles need a solid deck, and rotten decking gets replaced before anything goes over it.
  • Underlayment. Brava recommends a synthetic underlayment built to last as long as its tiles. DECRA requires an underlayment layer under its panels on every tear-off.
  • Fasteners. Brava's high-wind warranty calls for ring shank nails or screws, depending on the profile. The right fastener is part of the warranty.
  • Ventilation. Soffit intake and ridge exhaust keep a Texas attic from cooking the roof from underneath. Read why attic ventilation matters.

Know your warranties

  • The manufacturer's material warranty covers the product. DaVinci offers a lifetime limited material warranty. DECRA offers a lifetime limited warranty with coverage for 120 mph winds and hail penetration. Brava's 50-year material warranty doesn't require a certified installer, but its warranty that also covers workmanship is only available through Brava-certified installers.
  • KOG's labor warranty covers our work: five years on labor for roofs 4/12 and steeper. On a roof you pay for yourself, the KOG Straight Deal keeps it going up to 10 years while you keep the Roof Care Plan.

Before you sign with any roofer, ask which warranty you'll get in writing, and who you call if something goes wrong.

HOA and architectural approval

In a neighborhood with an HOA or architectural review, changing the roof material or color may need approval first. We give you the product name, color, and spec sheet for the request, so you can get the approval before the job is scheduled.

Insurance on a high-end roof

Texas insurers can give a premium credit for roofing rated for impact, and Class 4 gets the most. Each company sets its own amount, and some pair the credit with an exclusion for cosmetic hail damage, which means dents that don't cause leaks aren't covered. Ask your agent about both before you choose. When we install a roof rated for impact, we fill out and sign the state's Impact-Resistant Roofing Installation Form (PC068) for your insurance company.

Common questions

What's the best roof for a high-end home in North Texas?

It depends on the look you want. For the look of slate or cedar with hail protection, composite slate and shake rated Class 4 for impact. For clean, modern lines, standing seam metal. For the look of shake or tile in steel, stone-coated steel. We'll go over what fits your home and what each one costs.

How much does a composite slate or shake roof cost?

Our ballpark is $1,100 to $2,000 a square, torn off and installed, before deck work. A square is 100 square feet of roof, so a 32-square roof comes to roughly $35,200 to $64,000. Stone-coated steel runs about $900 to $1,500 a square, and standing seam $995 to $1,325. Your written price comes after we measure the roof.

Does a composite slate or shake roof need a new deck?

It needs a solid one. DaVinci requires solid wood panel or lumber sheathing. If your old roof was wood shakes on spaced boards, we tear off the wood and put down a new plywood deck first.

Will a luxury roof lower my insurance?

It can, if it's rated for impact. Texas insurers can give a premium credit for roofing rated Class 4, and each company sets its own amount. Some pair the credit with an exclusion for cosmetic hail damage, so ask your agent about both.
